To check ip: `hostname -I` (shd be the 10.89.xxx.xxx one)
To access RasPi5 via SSH:
At RasPi: `sudo service ssh start`
At Host: `ssh guinn@<ip_address>
-----------------------------------
Path for RasPi5 devices:
IMU = /dev/ttyUSB0
UART =  (GPIO) /dev/ttyAMA0
        (onboard) /dev/ttyAMA10 or /dev/serial0
JOYSTICK CONTROLLER = /dev/input/js0
CAN = /sys/bus/spi/devices/spi0.0/net/can0
check USB devices: lsusb
-----------------------------------
To display CAN device details:
ip -details -statistics link show can0
To send dummy message:
cansend can0 000#00.00 (or use PCAN and USB-to-CAN)
To show upcoming received message:
candump can0
